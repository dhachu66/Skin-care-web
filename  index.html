Timing guide
W1–2
Leave 1 hour only
Apply → leave 1 hour → rinse with plain cold water (no face wash) → apply Hydro Boost gel again → sleep.
W3–4
Leave 2–3 hours
Apply → wait 2–3 hours → rinse with plain cold water → apply Hydro Boost gel again → sleep.
M2+
Leave whole night
Apply → sleep with it on → do not rinse → wash off in morning with salicylic face wash as normal.
Golden rules
✕
Never apply on damp skin — wait full 30 mins after washing
✕
Never use more than pea size — more means irritation not results
✕
Never use on mask nights — Tuesday and Saturday
✕
Never skip sunscreen next morning — non negotiable
✓
Always moisturize BEFORE applying Tretinoin
✓
Always use cold water only on your face
✓
Only 3 nights per week for first 2 months — Mon, Wed, Fri
Severe burning, extreme peeling or swelling — rinse immediately, skip 2–3 nights. See a dermatologist if it continues.
Mild dryness, slight flaking, minor redness in week 1–2 is completely normal. Skin is adjusting to Tretinoin.
